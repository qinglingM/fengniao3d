Concept specimen 008

Make motion understandable.

OCTO / 08 is an original mechanical octopus concept. Eight articulated chains follow a traveling curvature wave. Armour, hydraulic rods and twin rows of suction cups remain independently inspectable.

Concept design: arm motion is a prescribed traveling wave, not a fluid or force simulation. The slider-crank diagram illustrates an ideal pump reference. Pressure, suction and underwater propulsion are not simulated.
